What we do here

Avengers Initiative RP is a community for people who love the Avengers universe and want to write in it together.

Op 1 // Roleplay

Play your favorite hero

Step into the roles of Earth's mightiest heroes, rivals and allies, and keep the banter, the stakes and the team dynamics alive in every scene.

Op 2 // Original characters

Bring your own OC

Have a character of your own? Build their backstory, powers and gear, then drop them into the Initiative alongside the icons.

Op 3 // Missions

Run missions and story arcs

Take on assignments, crises and cosmic-scale threats, written together by the whole team one reply at a time.

Op 4 // Community

Hang out with the team

Chat between scenes, share art and ideas, plan crossovers and find new people to write with.

Op 5 // Open to all

Every writer is welcome

New to roleplay or a seasoned storyteller, there is a place on the roster for you.
